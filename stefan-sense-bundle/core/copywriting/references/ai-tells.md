# Supplement: ai tells

Short supplement written for the Stefan Sense Bundle (the original reference file was not available).

Review inflated claims, empty contrasts, generic authority, repetitive closers, filler and invented detail.
For each change preserve the proposition, conditions, dates, quantities and certainty. Keep the author's voice.
Before delivery search for unsupported additions and dropped claims. A style marker alone is not proof of AI authorship.

## The self-check
Before delivery compare input and output claims. Search for unsupported numbers and testimonials, empty contrasts,
vague authority and repetitive closers. Read long sentences and the full draft aloud. Remove unsupported additions,
preserve caveats and mark missing proof as [NEED: ...]. Style flags alone do not establish AI authorship.
