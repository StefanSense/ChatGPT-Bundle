# Career

Rule: only real experience. Improve wording and structure; never invent employers, titles, dates, degrees, numbers or skills. Missing achievements → ask, or mark `[ADD RESULT: …]`.

## CV / resume tailored to a vacancy
1. Parse the vacancy: must-have vs nice-to-have skills, keywords, seniority signals.
2. Map the user's experience to requirements; show gaps honestly with suggestions.
3. Write: headline + 2–3 line summary for the target role; experience in reverse chronological order; bullets as **action + result + scale** ("Cut onboarding time from 14 to 5 days for 120 new hires/year" — only with the user's numbers); skills grouped; education/certificates.
4. ATS-friendly: standard headings, no tables/graphics for critical info, keywords used naturally, 1–2 pages.
5. Market conventions: e.g. photo usually omitted for US/UK; HH.ru profile structure for Russia; Europass for some EU applications — mention when relevant.

## Cover letter
Why this company (specific), why me (2–3 matching proofs), what I'll bring, call to action; under ~250 words unless asked.

## Interview preparation
- Likely questions: role-specific, behavioral, technical, about the company (web research if available).
- STAR answers drafted from the user's real stories (Situation, Task, Action, Result) — 1–2 minutes spoken.
- Questions to ask the interviewer. Mock interview on request: one question at a time, feedback after each answer.

## Salary negotiation
Market range only from sources the user provides or current web data (cite, with date); anchor, trade-offs (base, bonus, equity, remote, title), scripts for counter-offer. Respect that final numbers are the user's choice.

## Performance review / promotion case
Achievements with evidence, impact on goals, feedback received, growth areas, ask.

Deliver as DOCX/PDF (`documents`) when requested.
