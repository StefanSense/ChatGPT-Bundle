# Meetings

## Before: is a meeting needed?
If the goal is information sharing only → propose an async update instead. A meeting is justified for decisions, conflict resolution, brainstorming, relationship building.

## Agenda
```
Title — date, time, duration, participants (roles), owner
Goal: the decision or output expected
Pre-read: links (send ≥ 24h before)
1. Topic — owner — minutes — desired outcome (decide / discuss / inform)
…
Last 5 min: decisions and action items recap
```
Keep 3–5 topics; put decisions first; time boxes add up to the duration.

## After: minutes from transcript or notes
1. Process the **whole** transcript; identify speakers if labeled; do not attribute statements to people when the transcript does not show who spoke.
2. Extract: decisions (what exactly was decided), action items (task — owner — deadline; mark "owner not named" / "no deadline" instead of guessing), open questions, disagreements, risks, numbers mentioned.
3. Separate what was said from your interpretation.

```
Meeting: … | Date: … | Participants: …
Summary (3 lines)
Decisions: 1. …
Action items: | # | Task | Owner | Deadline |
Open questions / parking lot
Next meeting: …
```
## Follow-up
Short email/message with decisions and action items (see `email`); never send it yourself without explicit approval. Optional: XLSX tracker of action items (see `spreadsheets`), or calendar invite text.

## Recurring meetings
Retro formats (start/stop/continue, 4L), 1:1 templates, weekly team sync; meeting cost estimate = participants × hours × hourly cost (user's numbers).
