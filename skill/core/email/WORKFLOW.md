# Business email and messages

Goal: the recipient understands within the first two lines what is needed, from whom and by when.

## 1. Inputs
Recipient and relationship (colleague, client, boss, stranger), goal, key facts, deadline, desired tone, language, channel (email, Slack/Teams, Telegram/WhatsApp, LinkedIn). For replies: the full incoming message.
Do not ask about details you can leave as `[placeholder]`.

## 2. Structure
1. **Subject** (email): specific and scannable — "Approval needed: Q3 budget by Fri 12 Sep" / «Согласование бюджета Q3 до пт 12.09».
2. **First two lines**: the point or the request.
3. **Context**: only what the reader needs to act.
4. **Ask**: one clear request, owner, deadline; options if a decision is needed.
5. **Close**: next step; no filler ("Hope this finds you well" only where culturally expected).

## 3. Situations
- **Follow-up / reminder**: reference the previous message, restate the ask in one line, offer an easy path (yes/no, a slot).
- **Refusal**: decision first, short reason, alternative if any; no over-apologizing.
- **Apology / complaint response**: acknowledge specifically, own the part that is yours, state the fix and timeline; no blame-shifting, no admission of legal liability without the user's consent.
- **Negotiation**: anchor on interests, give a concrete proposal and a fallback; keep the door open.
- **Cold outreach**: relevance in line 1 (why them), one-sentence value, low-friction ask; under ~120 words; no fake familiarity, no invented mutual contacts or results.
- **Escalation**: facts and timeline, impact, what you need, by when.
- **Messenger**: shorter, no subject, one message — not five.

## 4. Tone controls
Formal ↔ friendly; direct ↔ soft. Russian business register: «Вы» with a capital letter in personal correspondence when formal; no bureaucratese (see `humanize-ru`). Cultural notes when writing across cultures (directness, honorifics) — mention them briefly if relevant.

## 5. Output
Subject + body ready to send; for important emails 2 variants (more formal / more direct). Placeholders `[…]` for unknown facts. Never send anything yourself: sending through a connector only after the user's explicit confirmation of the final text and recipients.
