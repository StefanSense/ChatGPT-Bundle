# Content strategy and plan

## 1. Inputs
Business goal, audience (use `product-marketing` context if attached), channels, resources (people, hours per week, video yes/no), existing content and what performed, competitors, period.

## 2. Strategy (one page)
1. Goal → measurable outcome (subscribers, leads, sales, hires) and the metric for each channel.
2. Audience questions and jobs: what they search, ask, argue about (from reviews, comments, sales calls; web search if available).
3. **Pillars** (3–5): intersection of expertise × audience needs × business goal.
4. **Formats per channel** and the role of each channel (discovery vs nurture vs conversion).
5. **Cadence** realistic for the resources; batching plan.
6. **Distribution**: repurposing chain (long piece → posts → newsletter → short video).
7. **Measurement**: weekly review — what to keep, change, stop.

## 3. Calendar
Table: date | channel | pillar | topic | format | hook/angle | CTA | owner | status. Mix: education, stories/cases, proof, engagement, offer (offers ≤ ~1 in 5 unless the user wants otherwise). Topics must be specific ("How we cut onboarding from 14 to 5 days" — only with the user's real data), not generic ("Tips about onboarding").

## 4. Output
Strategy page + calendar (inline table; XLSX via `spreadsheets` if asked) + 10–30 topic backlog with angles. Offer to draft the first pieces with `write` / `social`.
